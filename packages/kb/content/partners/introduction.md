## NodeByte Partnership Program
At NodeByte, we believe in fueling the builders, creators, and community leaders who push online spaces forward. Our Partnership Program is designed to build sustainable, mutually beneficial relationships. We don't just provide infrastructure support; we actively invest in your growth while you help expand the NodeByte ecosystem.

Whether you run a fast-growing gaming community, a content channel, or an open-source development project, we offer reliable performance infrastructure to keep your projects running smoothly.

---

## Partnership Tiers & Perks

We offer two distinct partnership tracks tailored to your community's focus. Our program shifts away from unmonitored free resource handouts, focusing instead on deep, sustainable infrastructure discounts and custom integrations to help you scale reliably.

| Track | Best For | Core Perks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Community & Creator** | Twitch Streamers, YouTubers, Gaming Communities, Event Organizers | • Up to 50% recurring discount on game/voice servers<br>• Custom promo code for your audience (10% off)<br>• Dedicated partner badge in the NodeByte Discord<br>• Cross-promotional support |
| **Developer & Project** | Modding frameworks, Game devs, Open-source tool creators, App devs | • High-performance VPS / isolated VM allocations at cost<br>• Alpha access to custom cloud gaming panel APIs<br>• Dedicated technical support channel<br>• Joint tech case studies & branding |

---

> **A Note on Sustainability:** NodeByte does not offer 100% free unmanaged resource pools. Our partnership model focuses on high-percentage, recurring discounts. This ensures all partner projects run on fully dedicated, premium isolated instances with guaranteed performance allocations, backed by real infrastructure stability.

### What We Expect From Our Partners
Partnership is a two-way street. We don't require intrusive ads, but we do look for active, organic integration:
* **Branding:** Clear placement of a "Powered by NodeByte" link or logo in your community channels (Discord sidebars, Twitch panels, GitHub READMEs).
* **Vibe Check:** Your community or project must maintain a constructive, healthy atmosphere that aligns with NodeByte’s community guidelines.
* **Engagement:** Natural, periodic engagement with NodeByte milestones or community updates.
