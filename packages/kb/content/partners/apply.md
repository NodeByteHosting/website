Ready to partner up? We handle our application pipeline with strict privacy and care. Follow the steps below to prepare your application and submit it to our management team.

---

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### **Step 1: Gather Your Analytics**
Collect screenshots or links to your public metrics from the last 30 days. This includes data dashboards such as Discord Server Insights, Twitch analytics dashboards, YouTube Studio summaries, or GitHub contribution graphs.

### **Step 2: Prepare Your Pitch**
Write a brief summary of your project or community. Be ready to explain:
* What you build, host, or stream.
* How you plan to utilize NodeByte’s high-performance infrastructure.
* How you plan to naturally showcase and integrate the NodeByte brand into your community.

### **Step 3: Submit Your Application**
Navigate to our official [Partnership Form](https://forms.gle/CknodbKGFZQNHRYf8) and fill out the detailed form. Ensure that your contact details especially your Discord username are completely accurate so we can reach you.

### **Step 4: Vibe Check & Technical Review**
Our management team will audit your submission. If your project passes the initial metric and community culture check, we will open a private text channel with you in the NodeByte Discord to discuss custom tier pricing, hardware allocations, and unique audience promo codes.

---

## The Review Process: What to Expect

Our internal audit takes approximately **3 to 5 business days**. Here is how we evaluate your submission behind the scenes:

### 1. Data Verification
We verify the authenticity of your channel or community metrics to ensure everything is organic. We look out for artificially inflated follower counts or botted Discord member numbers.

### 2. Infrastructure Assessment
Our tech team evaluates your exact requirements. We map out the necessary computational overhead (such as isolated virtual machine needs, memory footprints, or game panel allocations) to ensure we can guarantee your platform top-tier performance stability under our partner discount rates.

### 3. Final Decision
You will receive an email and a Discord ping indicating whether your application was **Approved, Deferred, or Declined**. 

* **If Deferred:** We will tell you exactly what benchmarks you need to hit or improve before re-applying (usually within 30-60 days).
